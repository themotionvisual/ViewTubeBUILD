Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 20, 2026

ViewTube ("ViewTube," "we," "us," or "our") operates the ViewTube service available at https://viewtube.live (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains how we access, use, store, process, share, and protect your information — including data obtained from Google APIs on your behalf. By using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

A. Google Account Information

When you sign in with your Google account, we request access to:

Data Scope Purpose
Profile info (name, email, photo) openid profile email Identify your account
YouTube channel & video data (read-only) youtube.readonly Identify your channel and read channel/video metadata, playlists, and inventory used throughout the analytics dashboard and creator workspace.
Comment Responder youtube.force-ssl Post creator-initiated replies to comments on your own YouTube content. YouTube's comments.insert operation requires this scope.
Upload Manager youtube.upload Upload videos you initiate from ViewTube's Upload Manager to your own YouTube channel.
YouTube Analytics (read-only) yt-analytics.readonly Retrieve performance metrics and dimensions — views, watch time, audience, engagement, and traffic sources — for dashboards and creator insights.
YouTube monetary analytics (read-only) yt-analytics-monetary.readonly Retrieve your own revenue and monetization metrics (estimated revenue, RPM where available) for display in the analytics dashboard. ViewTube does not modify monetary information.

B. Locally Stored Data

ViewTube renders most creator workflows in your web browser. The following are stored in your browser's local storage:

  • OAuth access tokens — Used to authenticate API requests to YouTube on your behalf. Tokens expire automatically (typically after 1 hour).
  • Cached analytics data — Your YouTube Analytics data may be cached in your browser to reduce API requests and improve performance.
  • User preferences — Settings such as your preferred Gemini API key, display preferences, and tool configurations.

C. Server-Side Handling

For features that require authenticated server operations — including the Authorization Code + PKCE OAuth flow and any user-initiated actions routed through ViewTube's servers — ViewTube may temporarily process Google-connected information on the server strictly to fulfill your request. Persisted OAuth credentials are encrypted at rest (see Section 5).

D. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect your browsing history or device fingerprints.
  • We do not use cookies for tracking or advertising purposes.
  • We do not sell Google user data.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the data accessed through Google APIs solely to:

  • Display your YouTube channel analytics in charts, dashboards, and reports within the Service.
  • Perform user-initiated YouTube management actions. When you explicitly invoke ViewTube's YouTube management tools, ViewTube may act on your YouTube account on your behalf. This includes uploading videos through the Upload Manager and posting comment replies through the Comment Responder. ViewTube does not perform these actions without a user-initiated feature or instruction.
  • Provide content creation tools such as thumbnail analysis, hook generation, and content strategy features.
  • Provide optional AI-powered creator features as described in Section 3.
  • Improve the Service by understanding which features are used (based on local usage data only, not transmitted to any server).

3. AI-Powered Features

ViewTube provides optional AI-powered creator features. When you intentionally invoke an AI feature, ViewTube may process relevant information from your connected YouTube account — including channel metadata, video metadata, analytics, comments, audience information, and monetary analytics where relevant — to produce the result you requested.

Depending on the selected ViewTube feature, information necessary to fulfill the request may be processed using Google AI services, including Gemini and supported Google generative media services such as Veo and Nano Banana.

ViewTube uses this processing only to provide prominent user-facing functionality you request. ViewTube does not sell Google user data, does not use it for advertising, and does not use Google user data to train a generalized AI or machine-learning model.

5. Data Protection and Security

ViewTube protects Google user data in transit and at rest using a defense-in-depth security architecture:

OAuth & Session Security

  • OAuth Authorization Code with PKCE (S256) for authorization requests.
  • OAuth state and nonce validation to prevent CSRF and replay.
  • HttpOnly session cookies with Secure in production and SameSite=Lax.
  • Trusted-origin validation on authorization callbacks.
  • Google API destination allow-listing so authenticated requests can only be sent to Google endpoints.
  • Cache-Control: no-store on responses that carry sensitive data, plus request timeouts.
  • Server-side secret/environment separation — credentials and API keys are never exposed to the browser bundle.

Credential Storage

  • Persisted Google OAuth credentials are encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM, keyed by the production ACCOUNT_TOKEN_ENCRYPTION_KEY.
  • Short-lived OAuth access tokens used by browser-side flows are stored in your browser's localStorage and expire automatically (typically after 1 hour).

Revocation & Deletion

  • ViewTube supports Google token revocation and account deletion flows so you can end ViewTube's access to your Google data.
  • You can also revoke ViewTube's access at any time from your Google Account Permissions page.

Third-Party API Keys

If you provide a Gemini API key for AI-powered features, that key is stored only in your browser's localStorage and is sent directly from your browser to Google's Gemini API.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information or YouTube data with third parties. Data may only be disclosed:

  • With your explicit consent.
  • To comply with legal obligations such as a valid court order, subpoena, or legal process.
  • To protect rights and safety if we believe disclosure is necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of ViewTube, our users, or the public.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

ViewTube retains account and Google-connected information for as long as necessary to provide the Service, maintain your authorized connection, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and protect the Service. You may revoke ViewTube's Google authorization through your Google Account at any time, and you may contact ViewTube using the details in Section 11 with a privacy or deletion request.

Local Browser Storage

  • Short-lived OAuth access tokens stored in your browser's localStorage expire automatically (typically after 1 hour).
  • Cached analytics data stored locally in your browser persists until you clear it or use the "Disconnect" / "Logout" feature.

Revoking Access

You can revoke ViewTube's access to your Google account at any time:

  1. Go to your Google Account Permissions page.
  2. Find "ViewTube" in the list of apps with access to your account.
  3. Click "Remove Access."

This invalidates ViewTube's access tokens and prevents ViewTube from accessing your YouTube data going forward.
