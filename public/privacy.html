Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ViewTube ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, stores, and discloses your information when you use the ViewTube web application at https://viewtube.live (the "Service"). By using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

A. Google Account Information

When you sign in with your Google account, we request access to:

Data Scope Purpose
Profile info (name, email, photo) openid profile email Identify your account
YouTube channel data youtube.readonly Read channel metadata, video lists, playlists
YouTube account management youtube youtube.force-ssl Edit video metadata, upload videos
YouTube Analytics yt-analytics.readonly View performance analytics and reports
YouTube monetary analytics yt-analytics-monetary.readonly View revenue and ad performance data

B. Locally Stored Data

ViewTube is a client-side web application. All data processing occurs within your web browser. We store the following in your browser's local storage:

  • OAuth access tokens — Used to authenticate API requests to YouTube on your behalf. Tokens expire automatically (typically after 1 hour).
  • Cached analytics data — Your YouTube Analytics data may be cached in your browser to reduce API requests and improve performance.
  • User preferences — Settings such as your preferred Gemini API key, display preferences, and tool configurations.

C. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not operate backend servers that receive, store, or process your YouTube data.
  • We do not store your Google access tokens on any server.
  • We do not collect your browsing history, IP address, or device fingerprints.
  • We do not use cookies for tracking or advertising purposes.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the data accessed through Google APIs solely to:

  • Display your YouTube channel analytics in charts, dashboards, and reports within the Service.
  • Enable video management features such as editing video metadata, uploading videos, and managing playlists.
  • Provide content creation tools such as thumbnail analysis, hook generation, and content strategy features.
  • Improve the Service by understanding which features are used (based on local usage data only, not transmitted to any server).

3. Google API Services User Data Policy

ViewTube's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Limited Use Disclosure

In accordance with Google's Limited Use requirements, ViewTube:

  1. Only uses Google user data to provide and improve user-facing features that are prominent in the application's user interface.
  2. Does not transfer Google user data to third parties unless necessary to provide or improve user-facing features, you provide affirmative consent, or it is necessary for security or legal compliance.
  3. Does not use Google user data for advertising, including retargeting, personalized advertising, or interest-based advertising.
  4. Does not allow humans to read Google user data unless we have your affirmative agreement for specific data, it is necessary for security purposes, or it is required by applicable law.
  5. Does not use Google user data to train generalized AI or machine learning models that are not related to providing or improving user-facing features of the Service.

4. Data Storage and Security

Client-Side Architecture

ViewTube operates entirely in your web browser. Your YouTube data:

  • Is fetched directly from Google's APIs by your browser.
  • Is processed and rendered locally in your browser.
  • Is cached in your browser's localStorage for performance.
  • Is never transmitted to ViewTube servers or any third-party servers.

Access Token Security

  • OAuth access tokens are stored in your browser's localStorage and expire automatically.
  • Tokens are used only to make authenticated requests to Google APIs from your browser.
  • No refresh tokens are stored server-side.

Third-Party API Keys

If you provide a Gemini API key for AI-powered features, that key is stored only in your browser's localStorage and is sent directly from your browser to Google's Gemini API. ViewTube never transmits or stores your API keys on any server.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information or YouTube data with third parties. Data may only be disclosed:

  • With your explicit consent.
  • To comply with legal obligations such as a valid court order, subpoena, or legal process.
  • To protect rights and safety if we believe disclosure is necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of ViewTube, our users, or the public.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Since all data is stored locally in your browser:

  • You control your data. You can clear all ViewTube data at any time by clearing your browser's local storage for viewtube.live, or by using the "Disconnect" / "Logout" feature within the Service.
  • Access tokens expire automatically (typically after 1 hour). Expired tokens are not usable.
  • Cached analytics data persists in local storage until you clear it or log out.

Revoking Access

You can revoke ViewTube's access to your Google account at any time:

  1. Go to your Google Account Permissions page.
  2. Find "ViewTube" in the list of apps with access to your account.
  3. Click "Remove Access."

This immediately invalidates all access tokens and prevents ViewTube from accessing your YouTube data.

If we become aware that a child has provided us with personal information, we will take steps to remove such information.
