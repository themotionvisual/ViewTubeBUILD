## Dashboard — 10 ideas

1. **IDEA-DASH-001 — Creator Command Center** — One master module showing channel health, active projects, urgent tasks, publishing pipeline, anomalies, opportunities, AI recommendations, and recent changes.
2. **IDEA-DASH-002 — Opportunity Radar** — Continuously surfaces unusually strong topics, videos, formats, traffic sources, search terms, audience behaviors, and content gaps worth investigating.
3. **IDEA-DASH-003 — Channel Pulse** — A compact real-time health instrument combining views, watch time, subscribers, revenue, CTR, retention, publishing frequency, and momentum versus normal ranges.
4. **IDEA-DASH-004 — Anomaly Monitor** — Detects significant deviations across videos and channel metrics and explains what changed, when, and possible contributing signals.
5. **IDEA-DASH-005 — Today / This Week Workspace** — Turns analytics, projects, tasks, comments, deadlines, and publishing plans into a prioritized operational view.
6. **IDEA-DASH-006 — AI Brain Briefing** — Generates a channel-aware briefing from Channel Knowledge, Evidence, projects, historical performance, audience behavior, and current objectives.
7. **IDEA-DASH-007 — Content Pipeline Visualizer** — Shows every piece of content moving through Idea → Research → Script → Production → Edit → Package → Publish → Measure → Learn.
8. **IDEA-DASH-008 — Goal & Forecast Tracker** — Tracks user-defined goals such as subscribers, uploads, revenue, watch hours, project completion, or content cadence and shows progress trajectories without replacing measured data.
9. **IDEA-DASH-009 — Recent Changes / Activity Ledger** — Unified log/projection of uploads, edits, AI generations, analytics changes, project actions, Vault additions, tests, and system activity.
10. **IDEA-DASH-010 — Dashboard Composer** — Full widget layout editor allowing saved dashboards for Channel, Production, Revenue, Shorts, Research, Projects, or custom workflows.

## Studio — 10 ideas

1. **IDEA-STUDIO-001 — Unified Content Builder** — A central creation environment connecting idea generation, research, scripting, assets, video generation, editing, packaging, publishing, and measurement.
2. **IDEA-STUDIO-002 — AI Director** — Converts a concept or script into scenes, shot lists, visual prompts, camera directions, transitions, audio ideas, pacing, and generation instructions.
3. **IDEA-STUDIO-003 — Research Workbench** — Gather sources, evidence, quotes, images, references, competing videos, historical materials, notes, and citations into a project-aware research workspace.
4. **IDEA-STUDIO-004 — Script Studio** — Channel-aware writing environment with structure analysis, hooks, pacing, scene links, claims/evidence, narration timing, revisions, and AI assistance.
5. **IDEA-STUDIO-005 — Video Generation Console** — Unified interface for image-to-video, text-to-video, avatar, B-roll, animation, Remotion, voice, music, and batch generation providers.
6. **IDEA-STUDIO-006 — Thumbnail Studio** — Thumbnail ideation, generation, composition, title pairing, variants, historical-performance context, and experiment management.
7. **IDEA-STUDIO-007 — Packaging Studio** — Build title, description, tags, chapters, thumbnail, pinned comment, community post, Shorts derivatives, end screens, and publishing metadata together.
8. **IDEA-STUDIO-008 — Asset Engine** — Automatically determines what assets a project requires, finds existing assets, identifies missing ones, and generates or requests what is still needed.
9. **IDEA-STUDIO-009 — Content Repurposing Lab** — Turn a long-form project into Shorts, clips, social posts, community posts, alternate cuts, images, quotes, or follow-up content.
10. **IDEA-STUDIO-010 — Production Recipe System** — Save reusable creation workflows such as historical Shorts, long documentaries, tutorials, and other formats including tools, prompts, stages, presets, and expected assets.

1. **IDEA-AN-001 — Analytics Explorer** — Flexible query workspace capable of comparing supported metrics, dimensions, time periods, formats, traffic sources, geography, or project/video scope.
2. **IDEA-AN-002 — Performance Decomposition** — Explain changes by breaking growth or decline into impressions, CTR, retention, traffic sources, returning viewers, geography, format, and other measurable contributors.
3. **IDEA-AN-003 — Audience Journey Map** — Show supported viewer paths from discovery through watching, continuation, subscription, return, playlists, or exit while marking unavailable transitions.
4. **IDEA-AN-004 — Video Lifecycle Analyzer** — Visualize Launch → Growth → Plateau → Revival → Long-tail behavior and compare lifecycle shapes across videos.
5. **IDEA-AN-005 — Retention Intelligence Lab** — Detect hooks, dips, spikes, rewatch points, abandonment zones, chapter effects, duration patterns, and recurring retention structures.
6. **IDEA-AN-006 — Traffic Intelligence** — Deep explorer for browse, suggested, search, Shorts feed, external, playlists, channel pages, notifications, end screens, and related supported sources.
7. **IDEA-AN-007 — Content Pattern Discovery** — Find correlations between topics, lengths, styles, titles, thumbnail traits, upload timing, structure, formats, and measured performance while distinguishing correlation from causation.
8. **IDEA-AN-008 — Comparative Cohorts** — Build groups such as topic families, Shorts under 30 seconds, tutorials, videos over an impression threshold, or time-based upload cohorts and compare them.
9. **IDEA-AN-009 — Experiment Analytics** — Central ledger/view for thumbnail, title, format, workflow, publishing, or content experiments with hypotheses, measured results, and evidence.
10. **IDEA-AN-010 — Analytics-to-Brain Learning Pipeline** — Convert validated analytical findings into structured evidence and learning candidates that BrainRuntime and Channel Knowledge can retrieve later.

## Consolidation architecture — 5 ideas

These are not proposals for five new persistence owners. They are consolidation lenses for grouping the 80 surface ideas onto existing canonical capabilities.

1. **IDEA-ARCH-001 — Intelligence Layer** — Unify AI Brain, Creator Context/Channel Knowledge, Evidence & Intelligence, specialist intelligence, analytics learning, Project Memory, and governed outcomes/learning behind existing owners.
2. **IDEA-ARCH-002 — ContentBuild Layer** — Treat Idea → Research → Script → Assets → Production → Edit → Packaging → Publish → Performance → Learning as one continuous Project/ContentBuild lifecycle.
3. **IDEA-ARCH-003 — Asset Layer** — Unify Vault, Asset Engine, generations, provenance, version families, project relationships, render outputs, and publication asset identity.
4. **IDEA-ARCH-004 — Measurement Layer** — Connect analytics, anomalies, experiments, opportunities, outcomes, cohorts, and evidence without creating parallel analytics truth.
5. **IDEA-ARCH-005 — Orchestration Layer** — Connect Projects, tasks, operations, automation, workflows, integrations, approvals, notifications, and system state through shared contracts.

## Source-level consolidation principle

The 80 page ideas should make ViewTube feel broader without creating 80 independent systems. Dashboard widgets, Studio tools, Project views, Analytics workbenches, Editor assistants, Vault utilities, Settings controls and Guide experiences should project or orchestrate canonical capabilities rather than become new truth stores.

Examples:
- Opportunity Radar on Dashboard should project the same Opportunity Intelligence used by Analytics, Projects, Studio and Brain.
- Project Memory, Channel Knowledge, Evidence, Creator Profile and AI context should remain distinct schemas/owners but converge through a shared intelligence/context architecture rather than five competing memories.
- Asset Engine, Projects, content packaging and publishing should preserve one Project/ContentBuild/asset identity chain.
- Analytics-derived learning should create evidence/learning candidates, not silently mutate model memory.
